    Clock

    How the time itself looks and where it sits.

    The Gregorian and Hebrew date lines stay attached to the clock and move with it. The top gap is ignored while the clock is centered or fitting to screen.

    Gregorian date

    The everyday date line shown under the clock.

    Tokens: D DD M MM MMM MMMM YY YYYY ddd dddd W WW H HH h hh m mm s ss A. This is a safe pattern, not an Excel formula. Add 'WW' WW to put work week on the same date line. Quote literal words. Sunday weeks use the Israeli work-week count: the week that starts on Sunday gets the number of the ISO week that starts the next day (Sunday 1 Nov 2026 is week 45; ISO says 44). Both the week line and W/WW date tokens use this rule.

    Hebrew date

    The Hebrew date line.

    Tokens: HW weekday (יום ראשון), HD day letters (כ״א), HDD day number, HM month, HB month with ב (בחשון), HYY year letters (תשפ״ז), HYYYY year number. Quote literal words.

    Place & Jewish calendar

    Location drives the time zone, holidays, Shabbat times and sun events.

    Changing city sets its time zone and Israel/Diaspora calendar region. A custom time zone changes the displayed time, not the city's coordinates. Hebrew dates, holidays, solar events and the Torah portion are displayed in Hebrew. Use {name} where the portion name should appear. Used when the format above is Custom. Use {in} for candle-lighting time and {out} for Shabbat end. Used when the format above is Custom.
    Show the Torah portion on these days
    Days follow the selected city's time zone. A Jewish holiday still appears on its own day.
    Color and bold for Jewish calendar items Tick “Own color” to give an item its own color instead of the general text color. Choosing a color ticks it for you. “Bold” makes that item heavier.

    Notes

    Write short notes and tick the days each one appears on, for example a reminder before Shabbat. A note can also replace the Place & Jewish calendar information (Torah portion, Shabbat times and holiday) on its days. Leave a note's text empty to turn it off.

    Up to 120 characters per note. Days follow the selected city's time zone. Style the notes under Appearance (“Note”).
